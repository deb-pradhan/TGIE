TGIE colour theme exploration

The same artwork from the live site, recoloured under seven candidate themes (the first is today’s). Each section shows the full palette, WCAG contrast checks, the hero and pillar illustrations, the ownership and lifecycle figures, and a small mock of the cell-collage UI. Nothing here is applied to the site yet.

Click a theme to jump to it. In themes with two accent hues, the illustrations use the second as a highlight colour (paths, sparks, connectors); the site artwork would be re-authored to use it deliberately.
